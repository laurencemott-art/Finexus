How It Works

A focused process from brief to appointment

We work closely with businesses throughout the process — from understanding the business and its objectives, through targeted headhunting, interviews and negotiations, to securing the right candidate.

01 · Partner

Partner with and consult with businesses

We work as a trusted partner rather than simply taking a recruitment brief. We build an understanding of the business, its market, its people and where it wants to go.

02 · Understand

Learn the business, understand the needs and objectives

We take the time to understand what you want to achieve, the challenges you are facing and the type of individual who will make a genuine difference to the business.

03 · Search

Targeted headhunting to meet your needs and goals

We identify and approach relevant individuals across our specialist markets, using our knowledge of FX, payments and financial technology to create a focused and targeted search.

04 · Secure

Manage interviews, negotiations and secure the right candidate

We manage the process from initial conversations through interviews, feedback, negotiations and offer management — keeping both sides aligned until the right appointment is secured.
