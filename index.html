The Process

A focused approach to finding the right people

We work closely with businesses from the initial conversation through to securing the right candidate.

01

Partner with and consult with businesses

We begin by partnering closely with the business, taking the time to understand the wider context behind the requirement and providing informed recruitment advice.

02

Learn the business and understand the need

We learn how the business operates, understand its needs and objectives, and establish exactly what it wants to achieve. This gives us the clarity needed to define the right profile.

03

Targeted headhunting

With the brief and objectives clearly defined, we undertake targeted headhunting to identify individuals whose experience, capabilities and ambitions align with the business and its goals.

04

Manage interviews, negotiations and securing the right candidate

We manage the process through interviews, feedback and negotiations, maintaining momentum and supporting both sides through to securing the right candidate.
